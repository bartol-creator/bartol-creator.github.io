Iron & Ink

Lead one of the nations of 1936 or 1939 through the Second World War: industry, research, diplomacy and armies across six thousand provinces, with the news of the day printed from your own country's point of view.

The quickest way to play is in your browser. The downloads are the same game, running a little faster on big wars. Saves don't move between the browser and the downloads.

How to install

There's no installer. You unpack a folder and run the game from it. Your computer will probably warn you the first time, because this is a free indie game that isn't registered with Microsoft or Apple. These steps get you past that.

  1. Download IronAndInk-win-x64.zip.
  2. Right-click the file in your Downloads folder and choose Extract All…, then Extract. Don't run the game from inside the zip, because it won't find its files.
  3. Open the new folder and double-click Iron & Ink.exe.
  4. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

To uninstall

Delete the folder. Saves and settings are in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Baki Games\Iron & Ink, and you can delete that folder too.

  1. Download the version for your Mac. To check which one you have, open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon, and "Processor: …Intel…" means Intel. Safari usually unpacks the zip for you. If it doesn't, double-click it.
  2. Drag Iron & Ink.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Double-click it. macOS will refuse to open it and say it can't check the app for malware. Click Done or OK.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to the Security section. Next to the message about Iron & Ink, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  5. From now on it opens normally.

If it says the app "is damaged"

Open Terminal, paste this line and press Return, then open the game again:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Iron & Ink.app"

To uninstall

Drag the app to the Trash. Saves and settings are in ~/Library/Application Support/com.bartolkaluder.ironandink, and you can delete that folder too.

Controls

Move the mapWASD or arrow keys, the middle mouse button, or the screen edges
ZoomMouse wheel (zoom far out for the paper map)
Select a country or provinceLeft click (a card with the country appears)
Diplomacy with a countryRight-click it, or E
Select and move divisionsLeft click their counter, then right-click a province
Pause, speedSpace, 1 to 6, + and −
ScreensThe menu strip at the top, or each screen’s key (shown when you point at it): F focus, R research, T trade, C construction, M telegrams and more
City ViewDouble-click a city
Menu, save, settings, quit to titleEsc (F5 quicksave, F9 quickload)

All keys can be changed in the game's settings.
